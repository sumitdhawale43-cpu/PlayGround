## ⚔️ LAST KINGDOM — 2D Tower Defense

Defend the kingdom from incoming enemy waves in this complete 2D tower defense game.

### Features

- **Four tower types** — 🏹 Archer, 🛡️ Spear Guard, 🔥 Flame Acolyte, 🎯 Ballista, each with its own placement cost, damage, range, fire rate and attack animation.
- **Multiple enemy types** — Militia, Spearman, Knight, Brute and the terrifying **Boss** enemies (with enrage phase).
- **Tower upgrades** — Between waves the Royal Treasury offers upgrade decrees that strengthen placed towers (+50% damage, +15% range, +35% fire rate).
- **Enemy wave progression** — 12 escalating waves across 3 challenging maps, with boss waves at the end of every third act.
- **Gold & resource management** — Earn gold from kills, spend it on towers, upgrades and continue to the next wave.
- **Different attack animations** — Archer draw & release, Spear Guard swing, Acolyte fire cast, Ballista reload.
- **Boss enemies** — Massive bosses with enlarged sprites, HP bars, crowns and a rage phase.
- **Three challenging maps** — The Fords, The Old Forest, The Black Citadel.
- **Health & wave indicators** — Kingdom hearts, wave counter, enemies remaining and gold HUD.
- **Mobile-friendly placement** — Tap a tower, then tap the field to place it; touch-ready controls.
- **Victory & defeat screens** — Campaign stats and restart options.

### Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder statically:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000 and press **⚔ BEGIN THE CAMPAIGN**.
