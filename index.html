How to Play

SURVIVE THE RIFT

01 Move & Jump

AD/←→move
SPACEWjump · double jump

Release jump early for a shorter hop. Land on ledges to refill your double jump.

02 Dash

SHIFTXdash

Cut through the air and shatter watchers. One dash per airtime — it recharges on the ground.

03 Phase

EFphase

Stand inside a portal and press Phase to cross dimensions. Portals may carry you somewhere far. Look for ghost platforms — they only exist on the other side.

04 The Two Worlds

Lumen is bright and weighty. Umbra is dark and floaty, with lower gravity — but its lava bites. Platforms, hazards, enemies and paths differ between worlds.

05 Secrets

Three relic keys are hidden in every rift, and some shards sit behind secret portals. Stars are earned by completing a rift, collecting shards, and finding every key.

06 Checkpoints

Touch a beacon to set your return point. Falling or touching a hazard sends you back — your collected shards stay with you.

Mobile: use the on-screen stick, then JUMP, DASH and PHASE.
