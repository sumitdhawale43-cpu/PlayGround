    How to build

    Three rules. Thirty circuits. No guesswork required.

    01

    Connect terminals

    Drag from one metal terminal to another. On touch screens, tap the first terminal then the second.

    02

    Toggle switches

    Tap a switch to open or close it. Switches and gates must genuinely control a live load.

    03

    Complete the circuit

    Power flows from +, through the lamps, and back to −. Watch the animated current and fix any short.

    CELL

    A battery makes a high rail (+) and ground (−).

    LAMP

    Current enters at A and leaves at K. Reverse it and nothing glows.

    SWITCH

    Closed passes power. Open breaks the path.

    LOGIC GATE

    Reads A/B and drives Y. AND needs both. OR needs one. XOR needs a mismatch. NOT flips.
