HOW TO PLAY

ARTILLERY

  • Drag from the battlefield to aim. Drag farther for more power, then release to fire.
  • A / D or ← / → to crawl. W / Space to jump. You have a limited move meter each turn.
  • Z bazooka · X grenade (3 bounces, short fuse).
  • Watch the wind. It pushes every shot sideways.

BUILDING

  • Q wall · E ramp · F floor. Click or drag to place pieces on the grid.
  • 1 / 2 / 3 pick wood, brick, or metal. Stronger material = more HP, more cost.
  • Blow up terrain and enemy builds to farm materials. Crates drop a jackpot.
  • Reaction build: while an enemy rocket is in the air, hit Space (or the big button) to slam down a wall and block the blast.
PRO TIP Walls don't just stop rockets — they cut blast damage to worms behind them. Build high ground, then rain down trouble.
